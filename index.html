About Me

A decade of turning complex learning challenges into impact.

I'm a Senior Instructional Design & QA Specialist and Certified Training Professional with over a decade of experience building learning systems that scale — from isolated rural classrooms in West Java to global corporate audiences in Melbourne, and to national teacher training programs spanning 20+ Indonesian provinces.

My work sits at the intersection of curriculum design, quality assurance, program evaluation, and systems thinking. I don't just build courses — I architect the frameworks, guidelines, feedback loops, and measurement tools that make entire learning ecosystems function at their best.

I've designed 150+ multi-modal courses, QA'd 600+ assets monthly, scripted 200+ e-learning videos, and led M&E for a bilateral Indonesia-Netherlands government program that scaled from 2 pilot schools to 60 vocational institutions nationwide. I've done this work with the U.S. Department of Defense, the Indonesian Ministry of Education, and a Melbourne-based global performance institute — always grounded in evidence, always oriented toward real human outcomes.

Beyond the institutional work, I founded Tim Brebes Mengabdi, a community NGO serving 250+ households in underserved villages — establishing an early childhood education center, advocating for infrastructure investment, and building something that outlasted my own involvement.

I bring a Fulbright Scholarship, a Penn State M.Ed. in Curriculum & Instruction, and the relentless conviction that the right learning design can change lives at any scale.
